Building drawings & symbols

5 cards · 4 quiz · 0 exam

Graphic Communication · National 5 · mrkellydt.co.uk

Types of building drawing

DrawingShowsTypical scale
Location planThe site in relation to the surrounding area: roads, neighbouring buildings. The site is outlined.1:1250 or 1:2500
Site planThe building within its site boundary: paths, drives, drainage, trees, services, north point, contours.1:200 or 1:500
Floor planRooms, walls, doors, windows and fittings on each floor.1:50 or 1:100
ElevationsThe outside of the building from each side.1:50 or 1:100
Sectional viewA cut through the building showing construction: foundations, walls, insulation, roof.1:20 or 1:50

British Standard symbols

Learn the symbols in the appendix of the course specification. Common ones: door (with the swing arc), window, WC, wash basin, sink, bath, shower, socket outlet, switch, lamp (light), radiator, stairs, north point, trees, contours, and section symbols for materials such as insulation, brick and concrete. Course reports say symbol questions are often poorly answered, so drill them.

What catches people out

  • Didn't know the British Standard symbols. Learn the appendix symbols: door, WC, lamp, socket, window and others. N5 2025

Key terms

Location planShows the site in its surrounding area; usually 1:1250 or 1:2500.
Site planBuilding within the site boundary, drainage, paths, trees, north point; usually 1:200 or 1:500.
Floor planRooms, doors, windows and fittings; usually 1:50 or 1:100.
North pointSymbol showing which way is north on a plan.
ContoursLines joining points of equal height, showing slope.

Quick check

1. Which drawing shows roads and neighbouring buildings around the site?

  1. Floor plan
  2. Location plan
  3. Sectional view
  4. Elevation
Show answer
B: Location plan. Location plan, 1:1250 or 1:2500.

2. A floor plan is usually drawn at…

  1. 1:2500
  2. 1:50 or 1:100
  3. 1:1
  4. 5:1
Show answer
B: 1:50 or 1:100. 1:50 or 1:100.

3. On a door symbol, the arc shows…

  1. A window
  2. Which way the door swings
  3. A radiator
  4. The stairs
Show answer
B: Which way the door swings. The swing.

4. Contour lines on a site plan show…

  1. Drainage
  2. Slope of the ground
  3. Boundaries
  4. Electricity
Show answer
B: Slope of the ground. Equal heights.
