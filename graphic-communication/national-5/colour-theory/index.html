Notes

The colour wheel

  • Primary: red, yellow, blue.
  • Secondary: orange, green, purple (violet), each made by mixing two primaries.
  • Tertiary: a primary mixed with a neighbouring secondary, e.g. red-orange, yellow-green, blue-violet, red-violet.

Using colour

  • Warm colours (reds, oranges, yellows) feel energetic and advance: they seem to come forward.
  • Cool colours (blues, greens, purples) feel calm and recede: they seem to sit back.
  • Harmony: colours next to each other on the wheel, giving a calm, unified look.
  • Contrast: colours opposite each other on the wheel (complementary), such as red and green or blue and orange, which make each other stand out.
  • Tint: a colour with white added (lighter). Shade: a colour with black added (darker).
  • Mood: colours suggest feelings: green for nature and health, red for danger or excitement, blue for trust or calm, black and gold for luxury.

Key terms

Primary colours
Red, yellow, blue.
Secondary colours
Orange, green, purple.
Tertiary colour
A primary mixed with a neighbouring secondary, e.g. red-orange.
Tint
A colour with white added.
Shade
A colour with black added.
Advancing colours
Warm colours that seem to come forward.
Receding colours
Cool colours that seem to sit back.
Harmonious colours
Neighbours on the colour wheel.
Contrasting colours
Opposites on the colour wheel.

Quick check

1. Which is a tertiary colour?

  1. Green
  2. Red-orange
  3. Blue
  4. Purple
Show answer
B: Red-orange. Primary + neighbouring secondary.

2. To make a tint you add…

  1. Black
  2. Grey
  3. White
  4. The complementary colour
Show answer
C: White. Tint = add white.

3. Red and green are…

  1. Harmonious
  2. Contrasting (complementary)
  3. Both cool
  4. Tertiary
Show answer
B: Contrasting (complementary). Opposite on the wheel.

4. Which colours seem to come forward?

  1. Cool colours
  2. Warm colours
  3. Tints only
  4. Greys
Show answer
B: Warm colours. Warm colours advance.

5. Blue, blue-green and green together are…

  1. Contrasting
  2. Harmonious
  3. Warm
  4. Primary
Show answer
B: Harmonious. Neighbours = harmony.
