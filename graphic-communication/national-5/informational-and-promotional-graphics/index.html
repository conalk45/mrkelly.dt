Graphs and charts

ChartUse it to
Bar chartCompare amounts between categories
Line graphShow change or a trend over time; help predict future values
Pie chartShow parts of a whole as percentages (all segments add up to 100%)
PictographShow amounts with repeated icons; eye-catching for a general audience

"Easy to read" doesn't score: give the specific purpose of that chart type.

Icons and symbols

Symbols and icons are understood across languages, take up less space than words, look modern and are quick to recognise. "Easy to read" on its own isn't accepted.

Promotional graphics

Posters, leaflets, magazine covers and pages, packaging, web pages, pull-up banners, menus and business cards. Effective promotional graphics have a clear focal point, a strong heading, consistent colours and fonts linked to the brand, and the key information (what, where, when, price, how to buy). Instructions such as assembly sheets use numbered stages, each in a box, with the parts needed shown next to each stage.

Key terms

Bar chartCompares amounts between categories.
Line graphShows a trend or change over time.
Pie chartShows parts of a whole; segments total 100%.
Benefit of iconsUnderstood across languages and take up less space.

  1. Pie chart

  3. Pictograph

2. A pie chart shows…

  1. Change over time
  2. Comparison of many categories over time
  3. Parts of a whole
  4. Exact values only
Show answer
C: Parts of a whole. Percentages of a total.
