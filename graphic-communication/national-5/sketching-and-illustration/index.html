Sketching & illustration

5 cards · 3 quiz · 0 exam

Graphic Communication · National 5 · mrkellydt.co.uk

Sketching skills

  • Proportion: sizes look right relative to each other. Compare widths and heights before you start.
  • Line quality: light construction lines first, then firmer, darker outlines. Construction lines must look different from outlines.
  • Crating: sketch a box the overall size, then cut away or add to it.
  • Vanishing points: for perspective, keep depth lines heading to the VPs.
  • From orthographic views: be able to sketch a pictorial from given elevations and plans, and orthographic views from a pictorial, in third angle with hidden detail.

Assignment sketching rules

  • No drawing boards, set squares or drawing equipment: using them gives 0 marks for the whole sketching task. A straight edge is allowed.
  • Digital sketching: straight edge tool only. No shape or circle tools, and no software that turns a 3D model into a 2D sketch.
  • Don't model the object in CAD and copy it: the task assesses your ability to interpret the object.
  • Show the elevation in the direction the data sheet indicates.

Illustration techniques

  • Light source: decide where the light comes from; faces towards it are lightest.
  • Tone: graded light to dark shading that shows form.
  • Shade and shadow: the dark side of the object, and the shadow it casts on the ground.
  • Reflection: streaks and highlights on shiny surfaces such as glass, metal and gloss plastic.
  • Texture and material: wood grain, brushed metal, fabric.
  • Gradient: a smooth change of colour or tone.

What catches people out

  • Assignment: hidden detail and proportion in orthographic sketches were weak. Practise related third-angle sketches with hidden detail, in proportion; no drawing boards. N5 2024, 2025

Key terms

CratingSketch a box the overall size first, then build the object inside it.
Construction linesLight lines used to set out a sketch; must look different from outlines.
ToneGraded shading from light to dark that shows form.
ReflectionHighlights or streaks that show a shiny surface.
Drawing board in the sketching taskNot allowed: 0 marks for the task.
