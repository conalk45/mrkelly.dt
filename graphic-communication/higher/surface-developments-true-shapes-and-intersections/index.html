Surface developments, true shapes & intersections

6 cards · 4 quiz · 0 exam

Surface developments

A surface development (a net) is the flat shape that folds up to make a 3D form. Packaging is designed this way.

  • Prism: a row of rectangles plus the two end shapes.
  • Cylinder: a rectangle whose length is the circumference (π × diameter) plus two circles.
  • Cone: a sector of a circle whose radius is the slant height.
  • Pyramid: triangles whose sloping edges are drawn true length.
  • When a form is cut at an angle, the development shows the cut line traced around the surface. Exam questions show several nets and ask which is correct: check the order of faces and where the cut falls.

Developments use fold lines and often glue tabs. To reduce waste and cost, nets are nested together on the sheet.

Shapes, forms and cuts

Forms you should recognise in any view: squares, rectangles, circles, hexagons and octagons; prisms, pyramids, cones and cylinders. Questions combine two forms or show a single cut (e.g. a cylinder cut at an angle gives an ellipse as the true shape).

Interpenetration and intersections HIGHER

When two solids join, such as a pipe meeting another pipe or a cylinder through a prism, the line where their surfaces meet is the line of intersection. It is found by dividing one form into strips (generators), projecting where each line meets the other surface into the other views, and joining the points with a smooth curve. Equal cylinders meeting at 90° give straight-line intersections in the elevation. The true shape of an oblique cut through a cylinder is an ellipse.

Key terms

Surface developmentThe flat net that folds to make a 3D form.
Cylinder developmentRectangle of length π × d, plus two circles.
Cone developmentA sector with radius equal to the slant height.
Glue tabExtra flap on a net to join edges.
Line of intersectionThe line where two joined solids' surfaces meet.
True shape of an angled cut through a cylinderAn ellipse.

Quick check

1. The length of a cylinder's development is…

  1. The diameter
  2. The radius
  3. π × diameter
  4. 2 × height
Show answer
C: π × diameter. The circumference.

2. A cone develops into…

  1. A rectangle
  2. A sector of a circle
  3. A triangle
  4. A square
Show answer
B: A sector of a circle. Radius = slant height.

3. Fitting as many nets as possible onto a sheet…

  1. Increases waste
  2. Reduces waste and cost
  3. Makes folding harder
  4. Is not allowed
Show answer
B: Reduces waste and cost. A sustainability point that scores.

4. A cylinder cut at an angle has a true shape that is…

  1. A circle
  2. An ellipse
  3. A rectangle
  4. A parabola
Show answer
B: An ellipse. An ellipse.
