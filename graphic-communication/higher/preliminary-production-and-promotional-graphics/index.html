Notes

Three types of graphic

TypeWhat it is forWho uses itExamples
PreliminaryGenerating, exploring and communicating early ideas quicklyThe designer, the design team, the clientThumbnails, freehand sketches, annotated ideas, sketch models, rough layouts
ProductionGiving accurate technical information so something can be made or builtManufacturers, engineers, builders, the construction tradeDimensioned orthographic and sectional views, assembly drawings, exploded views, building plans
PromotionalAdvertising or selling a product, service or eventCustomers, the public, the clientPosters, leaflets, magazine pages, packaging, web pages, pull-up banners, rendered CAD illustrations

Exam questions often show three graphics and ask you to describe the purpose of each. Name the type and link it to the actual product: "Figure 2 is a production graphic: it gives the builder the sizes and position of the windows" scores; "it is a production drawing" alone does not.

Manual or computer-generated?

  • Manual (pencil, marker, fineliner): quick, cheap, needs no equipment, good for early ideas and working with a client face to face; hard to edit, can't be reused or sent to a machine.
  • Computer-generated (CAD, DTP): accurate, easy to edit and reuse, can be shared electronically, rendered realistically and sent to CAM; needs hardware, software and training, and files can be lost or incompatible.
  • Digital sketching on a tablet mixes both: layers, undo, colour and sharing, with the speed of freehand.

Key terms

Preliminary graphic
Quick sketches and thumbnails used to generate and explore ideas, for the designer and client.
Production graphic
Accurate, dimensioned drawing to standards so the item can be manufactured or built.
Promotional graphic
A graphic that advertises or sells: posters, leaflets, packaging, web pages, renders.
Advantage of manual sketching
Fast, needs no equipment, good for sharing ideas with a client on the spot.
Advantage of CAD graphics
Accurate and easy to edit; can be shared, reused, rendered and sent to CAM.

Quick check

1. A fully dimensioned orthographic drawing for a manufacturer is a…

  1. Preliminary graphic
  2. Production graphic
  3. Promotional graphic
  4. Thumbnail
Show answer
B: Production graphic. It gives technical information for making the item.

2. Thumbnails and annotated freehand sketches are…

  1. Preliminary graphics
  2. Production graphics
  3. Promotional graphics
  4. Assembly drawings
Show answer
A: Preliminary graphics. They are used to generate and explore ideas.

3. A rendered image of a new car on a billboard is a…

  1. Production graphic
  2. Preliminary graphic
  3. Promotional graphic
  4. Sectional view
Show answer
C: Promotional graphic. It advertises the product to the public.

4. Which is a benefit of manual sketching over CAD?

  1. It is more accurate
  2. It can be sent straight to a 3D printer
  3. It is quick and needs no equipment
  4. It is easier to edit
Show answer
C: It is quick and needs no equipment. Sketching is fast and portable; CAD is more accurate and editable.
