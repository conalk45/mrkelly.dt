Notes

What it is

The Creative Thinking Qualification was developed by Daydream Believers, a group of employers, creative agencies, teachers, lecturers and students. It was written with Edinburgh College and credit-rated by Edinburgh Napier University.

  • Level 5 and Level 6 awards on the SCQF, each worth 24 credit points: the same size as a National 5 or Higher.
  • No exam: you build a portfolio (usually a sketchbook plus digital work) from projects set by real companies.
  • It's about the process, not the product. A brilliant final idea scores little if the thinking behind it isn't shown.

The five elements

Everything you do fits into five elements, and each one is assessed:

ElementWhat you show
ResearchYou explore the problem before jumping to ideas
ConceptsYou propose creative ideas that grow out of your research
Fail & FixYou test ideas, learn from what goes wrong, and improve them
CommunicateYou present your ideas clearly and creatively
EvaluationYou judge your own work honestly, with "creative bravery"

Level 5 and Level 6

Level 5Level 6
ResearchSecondary research to explore a problemPrimary and secondary research
CommunicateCommunicate ideas and expected outcomesCommunicate and evaluate ideas
Supporting subjectUsually taken alongside N5 EnglishUsually taken alongside Higher English

At Level 6 you're expected to go deeper: more independent research, more alternatives, sharper critical thinking.

How you are graded

Each element gets a score, and your grade (A–D) comes from all five together. Teachers often use a STAMP in your sketchbook to show where you are on each element as you go, so you can see what to improve.

Your work is assessed by your teacher and checked (verified) by the Daydream Believers team.

Projects (briefs)

You complete between one and four projects, from briefs written by industry partners. Past briefs have included designing a space like a hospital or music venue, a kindness advertising campaign, deciding how a community should use a donated campervan, and building a fashion brand for the circular economy.

Themes range across fashion, architecture, product design, engineering, photography and film, game design and advertising, so you can follow your own interests.

Quick check

1. Which is NOT one of the five elements?

  1. Research
  2. Fail & Fix
  3. Manufacture
  4. Evaluation
Show answer
C: Manufacture. The five are Research, Concepts, Fail & Fix, Communicate and Evaluation.

2. How is the qualification assessed?

  1. A two-hour exam
  2. A portfolio of project work
  3. A multiple-choice test
  4. An interview only
Show answer
B: A portfolio of project work. There is no exam.

3. What matters most in the portfolio?

  1. A perfect final product
  2. The thinking process shown throughout
  3. The number of pages
  4. Using expensive materials
Show answer
B: The thinking process shown throughout. Process over product.

4. How many SCQF credit points is each award worth?

  1. 6
  2. 12
  3. 24
  4. 48
Show answer
C: 24. 24 credits, the same size as a National Course.
