Notes

Personal protective equipment (PPE)

  • Apron: protects clothes and stops loose clothing catching.
  • Goggles, safety specs or visor: eye protection on machines and when chiselling, drilling or turning.
  • Dust mask / dust protection: when sanding, especially MDF.
  • Gloves: when applying stains, oils and finishes, but never on rotating machines like the lathe or drill.

Safe working in the workshop

  • Tie back long hair; tuck in ties and loose sleeves; remove jewellery.
  • Keep the floor and bench clear; walk, don't run.
  • Carry tools with sharp edges pointing down.
  • Hold work in a vice or with cramps, and keep both hands behind cutting edges.
  • Know where the emergency stops are, and use machines only when your teacher allows.
  • Report damaged tools and machines straight away.

Tool care

  • Honing chisels and plane irons on an oilstone or whetstone keeps them sharp: hold the bevel at the honing angle (about 30°), then remove the burr on the flat back.
  • Setting a plane: adjust the depth of cut with the adjusting nut and the lateral lever so the blade is parallel to the sole; retract the blade and lay the plane on its side after use.
  • Store chisels with edge guards; never use a chisel as a screwdriver.
  • Keep saws sharp and hang them up so teeth aren't damaged.

The log book (15 marks)

The log book is part of the practical activity:

SectionMarks
Machine care and maintenance5
Tool care and maintenance5
Safe working procedures (completed by your assessor)5
  • Write it yourself, in your own words: copying from exemplars, classmates or comment banks isn't allowed.
  • Use tools and machines from the course specification.
  • Use a different tool for each procedure you describe.
  • Give the detail of how you did each check or task, not just a list.

Quick check

1. When should gloves NOT be worn?

  1. Applying stain
  2. Using the woodturning lathe
  3. Applying oil
  4. Handling rough timber
Show answer
B: Using the woodturning lathe. Gloves can be pulled into rotating machines.

2. How many marks is the log book worth?

  1. 5
  2. 10
  3. 15
  4. 20
Show answer
C: 15. Machine care 5, tool care 5, safe working 5.

3. Who completes the safe working procedures section of the log book?

  1. The candidate
  2. The assessor
  3. A classmate
  4. SQA
Show answer
B: The assessor. Your assessor completes it.

4. What is honing?

  1. Sharpening a chisel or plane iron on a stone
  2. Sanding with fine paper
  3. Setting a plane depth
  4. Cleaning a machine
Show answer
A: Sharpening a chisel or plane iron on a stone. Honing gives a keen cutting edge.

5. Which PPE is needed when sanding MDF?

  1. Gloves
  2. Dust mask
  3. Ear defenders only
  4. None
Show answer
B: Dust mask. MDF dust is fine and harmful.
