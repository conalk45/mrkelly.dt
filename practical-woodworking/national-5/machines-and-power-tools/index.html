Portable power tools

  • Corded and cordless drills: drilling holes away from the pillar drill.
  • Cordless screwdriver: driving screws quickly with clutch control.
  • Jigsaw: cuts curves in sheet material and boards. Clamp the work, keep the cable clear of the blade, wait for the blade to stop.
  • Orbital sander: fine sanding of flat surfaces. Belt sander (portable): fast removal of material.

Before using a power tool: check the cable and plug for damage, check guards are fitted, check the blade or bit is tight and sharp, clamp the work, wear goggles and use dust extraction.

Machine care and maintenance

  • Report faults to your teacher straight away and don't use the machine.
  • Check cables for cuts and loose plugs.
  • Check guards are in place and working.
  • Check tool holding: chucks, collets and blades tight.
  • Use dust extraction, and clean the machine after use.

These go in your log book: describe them in your own words, for tools from the course specification.

Quick check

1. Which machine cuts square mortises?

  1. Pillar drill
  2. Mortise machine
  3. Disc sander
  4. Jigsaw
Show answer
B: Mortise machine. It uses a hollow square chisel with an auger inside.

2. Which side of a disc sander should you use?

  1. The upward-moving side
  2. The downward-moving side
  3. Either
  4. The edge
Show answer
B: The downward-moving side. The downward side pushes the work onto the table.

3. How should work be held on a pillar drill?

  1. By hand
  2. In a machine vice or clamped
  3. On your knee
  4. It doesn't matter
Show answer
B: In a machine vice or clamped. Work can spin if not clamped.

4. What is a safety check before using a jigsaw?

  1. Check the cable for damage
  2. Paint the blade
  3. Remove the guard
  4. Wet the wood
Show answer
A: Check the cable for damage. Damaged cables are an electric shock risk.
