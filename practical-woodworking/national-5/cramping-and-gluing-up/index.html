Notes

Types of cramp

CrampUse
Sash crampLong bar cramp for gluing up frames, carcases and wide boards
G-crampSmall jobs, holding work to the bench, and holding parts together
Mitre crampHolds mitre joints at 90° while the glue sets
Band (web) crampA strap that pulls irregular or many-sided shapes together

Put scrap blocks between the cramp jaws and the work to stop the jaws indenting (denting) the wood.

Dry cramping

A dry cramp is a trial assembly with no glue. It lets you:

  • check that every joint fits and closes up
  • check the frame is square (measure the diagonals: they should be equal)
  • set the cramps to the right size and have everything ready
  • fix problems before glue makes them permanent.

Gluing up

  1. Dry cramp and check.
  2. Apply glue (PVA) to both joint surfaces.
  3. Assemble and cramp, with scrap blocks under the jaws.
  4. Check for square: compare the diagonals and adjust the cramps if they differ.
  5. Wipe off excess glue with a damp cloth before it sets: dried glue stops stain and varnish taking.
  6. Leave to set. Check the cramps aren't bending the frame (winding).

Quick check

1. What is the purpose of dry cramping?

  1. To dry the glue faster
  2. To check the fit and squareness before gluing
  3. To stain the wood
  4. To clean the joints
Show answer
B: To check the fit and squareness before gluing. It's a rehearsal without glue.

2. How do you check a frame is square after cramping?

  1. Measure both diagonals
  2. Use a marking gauge
  3. Look at it
  4. Measure the width only
Show answer
A: Measure both diagonals. Equal diagonals mean the frame is square.

3. Which cramp would you use to glue a picture frame's mitres?

  1. Sash cramp
  2. Mitre or band cramp
  3. G-cramp only
  4. Bench vice
Show answer
B: Mitre or band cramp. Mitre and band cramps suit mitred frames.

4. How do you stop a G-cramp marking the wood?

  1. Tighten it more
  2. Use scrap blocks under the jaws
  3. Use more glue
  4. Wet the wood
Show answer
B: Use scrap blocks under the jaws. Scrap blocks spread the pressure.
