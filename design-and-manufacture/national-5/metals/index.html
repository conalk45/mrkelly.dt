Notes

Ferrous and non-ferrous

  • Ferrous: contain iron, attracted to magnets (except stainless steel), rust unless protected.
  • Non-ferrous: no iron, not magnetic, form a dense oxide layer (tarnish) that slows further corrosion.
  • Alloy: a mixture of a metal with other elements to improve properties (brass = copper + zinc; bronze = copper + tin; stainless steel = steel + chromium).

Key terms

Ferrous metal
Contains iron; magnetic (except stainless); rusts.
Alloy
A metal mixed with other elements to improve its properties.
Mild steel
Ductile, malleable, easily welded. Car bodies, nuts and bolts.
Stainless steel
Corrosion resistant and hygienic. Cutlery, sinks.
Aluminium
Lightweight with good strength-to-weight ratio. Cans, ladders, aircraft.
Brass
Copper + zinc alloy: polishes well, corrosion resistant. Taps, fittings.
Copper
Excellent electrical conductor. Wires, pipes.

Quick check

1. Which metal is ferrous?

  1. Aluminium
  2. Brass
  3. Mild steel
  4. Copper
Show answer
C: Mild steel. Steel is mostly iron.

2. Brass is an alloy of copper and…

  1. Tin
  2. Zinc
  3. Iron
  4. Aluminium
Show answer
B: Zinc. Copper + zinc.

3. Which metal suits kitchen cutlery?

  1. Mild steel
  2. Cast iron
  3. Stainless steel
  4. Copper
Show answer
C: Stainless steel. Corrosion resistant and hygienic.

4. Why is aluminium used for drinks cans?

  1. It is magnetic
  2. Lightweight and corrosion resistant
  3. It is brittle
  4. It is cheapest metal
Show answer
B: Lightweight and corrosion resistant. Light and does not rust.

5. Cast iron is unsuitable for a product that must bend because it is…

  1. Ductile
  2. Brittle
  3. Malleable
  4. Lightweight
Show answer
B: Brittle. Brittle materials snap rather than bend.

Exam-style questions

1. The frame of an outdoor bench is stainless steel. Explain why this is suitable. (2 marks)

Show marking guidance
  • Corrosion resistant so it will not rust outdoors (1)
  • Strong/tough so it supports the users' weight (1)
  • Attractive finish needing little maintenance (1)
