Notes

N5: who influences design

  • Designers: creative input; develop ideas to meet the brief.
  • Manufacturers: make sure it can be manufactured economically and efficiently; advise on changes.
  • Marketing teams: plan how to promote and sell the product (price, promotion, product, place).
  • Consumers: give feedback (often via market research) on needs, wants and existing products.
  • Retailers: feedback on sales figures, what is likely to sell and market trends.

Key terms

Role of a retailer
Feedback on sales, what will sell and market trends.
Role of a manufacturer
Ensures the product can be made efficiently and economically.
Role of a marketing team
Plans how the product is priced, promoted and sold.
