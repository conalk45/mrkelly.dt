Notes

Two groups of plastic

  • Thermoplastics: soften when heated and can be reshaped repeatedly ("plastic memory"). Recyclable.
  • Thermosetting plastics: set permanently when first heated and shaped; can't be softened again. Heat resistant.

Plastics are lightweight, corrosion resistant, insulate heat and electricity, come in any colour, and suit mass production.

Plastics you need to know

Composites and elastomers

  • Composite: two or more materials combined (a matrix, usually resin, holding a reinforcer such as fibres) to give improved properties.
    • GRP (glass-reinforced plastic): light, strong, easily formed. Boat hulls, car panels, surfboards.
    • Carbon-fibre plastic: very high stiffness and strength-to-weight, but expensive. Bikes, F1, aerospace.
  • Elastomers (rubber, silicone): flexible, non-slip, water and impact resistant. Seals, grips, wheels, utensils.
  • Identifying materials: look and feel, recycling symbols and labels, tests (float, magnet, flexibility), comparison with known samples.
  • New materials (e.g. bio-composites, graphene) change what products can do, how they're made and their environmental impact.

Key terms

Thermoplastic
Softens when heated; can be reshaped and recycled.
Thermosetting plastic
Sets permanently once heated; heat resistant; cannot be reshaped.
ABS
Tough, impact resistant, high gloss. Toys, casings, helmets.
Acrylic
Rigid, clear, polishes well. Glass substitute, signs.
Polypropylene
Fatigue resistant (living hinges), chemical resistant. Caps, crates.
Urea formaldehyde
Thermoset electrical insulator. Plugs and sockets.
Melamine formaldehyde
Hard, scratch and heat resistant. Laminates, picnic ware.
Nylon
Self-lubricating and wear resistant. Gears, bearings.
Composite
Matrix + reinforcer combined for better properties, e.g. GRP, carbon fibre.
Elastomer
Flexible, grippy material like rubber or silicone.

Quick check

1. Which plastic is used for electrical plugs?

  1. Polystyrene
  2. Urea formaldehyde
  3. Acrylic
  4. Polypropylene
Show answer
B: Urea formaldehyde. Thermoset that insulates and resists heat.

2. A flip-top shampoo lid with a living hinge is made from…

  1. Acrylic
  2. Polypropylene
  3. Melamine
  4. Polystyrene
Show answer
B: Polypropylene. Polypropylene resists fatigue.

3. Which can be reheated and reshaped?

  1. Melamine formaldehyde
  2. Urea formaldehyde
  3. Acrylic
  4. Bakelite
Show answer
C: Acrylic. Acrylic is a thermoplastic.

4. Which is the best substitute for glass in a light cover?

  1. ABS
  2. Acrylic
  3. MDF
  4. Polystyrene foam
Show answer
B: Acrylic. Transparent and shatter resistant compared to glass.

5. Why is nylon used for small gears?

  1. It is transparent
  2. It is self-lubricating and wear resistant
  3. It is a thermoset
  4. It is cheap foam
Show answer
B: It is self-lubricating and wear resistant. Low friction and hard wearing.

6. Carbon-fibre plastic is chosen for racing bikes mainly because of its…

  1. Low cost
  2. High strength-to-weight ratio
  3. Flexibility
  4. Transparency
Show answer
B: High strength-to-weight ratio. Very strong and stiff but light.

Exam-style questions

1. Describe two advantages of plastics over wood and metal for a product such as a kettle. (2 marks)

Show marking guidance
  • Can be moulded into complex forms in one piece (1)
  • Electrical/heat insulator so safe to hold (1)
  • Range of colours with no finishing needed (1)
  • Water/corrosion resistant (1)

2. Describe two methods used to identify the materials in commercially manufactured products. (2 marks)

Show marking guidance
  • Identification symbols/recycling codes on the part (1)
  • Tests: magnet test, float test, flexibility (1)
  • Look and feel / comparison with known samples (1)
