The design team HIGHER

MemberRole
DesignerCreative input; increasingly manages product development.
ErgonomistAnthropometric, physiological and psychological data for the target users.
LawyerLegal compliance in the country of sale; protects IP (patents, trademarks) and avoids infringing others'.
Production engineerProduction rate, volume and best production system; tooling and planning.
Project managerPlans and oversees tasks so the project runs to time and budget.
Market researcherFinds consumer needs and wants, pricing, trends, projected sales.
Materials technologistAdvises on material properties and suitable manufacturing methods.

Good communication between members (meetings, shared CAD files, reports) prevents costly mistakes.

Communicating in the team: regular meetings and video calls, shared CAD files and cloud folders, reports and specifications, Gantt charts so everyone sees deadlines, and models or prototypes to discuss physical issues. Poor communication causes delays and costly mistakes.

In-house team vs sub-contracting HIGHER

AdvantagesDisadvantages
In-houseAlways available; know company practices; reliable; less risk of ideas leaking to competitorsPaid even when not needed; may lack specialist skills; ideas can become stale
Sub-contractingSpecialist expertise; fresh ideas; only hired when needed; flexibleHigher fees; may be unavailable or unreliable; risk of sharing information; IPR issues

Key terms

Role of a retailerFeedback on sales, what will sell and market trends.
Role of a manufacturerEnsures the product can be made efficiently and economically.
Role of a marketing teamPlans how the product is priced, promoted and sold.
Materials technologistAdvises on material properties and suitable manufacturing methods.
Production engineerPlans production rate, volume, tooling and system.
Lawyer (design team)Legal compliance and protection of intellectual property.
Advantage of sub-contractingAccess to specialist skills and fresh ideas, only paid when needed.

Quick check

1. Who gives feedback on which products are selling well in shops?

  1. Retailer
  2. Materials technologist
  3. Ergonomist
  4. Lawyer
Show answer
A: Retailer. Retailers see sales directly.

2. Who checks a design can be made efficiently and economically?

  1. Consumer
  2. Manufacturer
  3. Marketing team
  4. Retailer
Show answer
B: Manufacturer. Manufacturers advise on how to make it.

3. Who would apply for a patent on a new mechanism?

  1. Project manager
  2. Lawyer
  3. Ergonomist
  4. Market researcher
Show answer
B: Lawyer. Lawyers protect intellectual property.

4. Which is a disadvantage of using an external designer?

  1. Fresh ideas
  2. Specialist skills
  3. Higher fees and IPR issues
  4. Only hired when needed
Show answer
C: Higher fees and IPR issues. Consultants cost more and may claim rights.

5. Who provides data on hand grip sizes for a target market?

  1. Ergonomist
  2. Lawyer
  3. Production engineer
  4. Retailer
Show answer
A: Ergonomist. Ergonomists supply anthropometric data.

Exam-style questions

1. A toy manufacturer employed an external designer. Explain one advantage and one disadvantage of this. (2 marks)

Show marking guidance
  • Advantage: only hired when needed, saving money; fresh ideas; specialist knowledge (1)
  • Disadvantage: higher fees; unfamiliar with company practices; IPR issues / risk of leaks (1)

2. Describe the influence two people from the design team have on the design of products. (4 marks)

Show marking guidance
  • 1 mark per valid description, max 3 per person
  • e.g. Materials technologist advises on suitable materials for the target market (1) and works with production engineers so materials suit the processes available (1)
  • e.g. Market researcher works with focus groups to find needs/wants and acceptable price (1)
