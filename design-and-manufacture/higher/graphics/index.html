Graphics through the design process

Sketches are the quickest way to communicate design work. The type and quality of graphic depends on the stage and on who will see it.

GraphicPurpose
Annotated sketchesQuickly record and develop ideas
Orthographic sketch/drawingShow dimensions and proportions
Isometric, oblique, perspective3D view to visualise an idea or share with non-experts
Sectional viewShow interior detail
Exploded viewShow how components fit together
Scale drawingCheck size, proportion and fit
Working drawingGive the manufacturer every view, dimension and detail needed
Rendered illustration / CAD renderCommunicate the final proposal to the client

Key terms

Sectional view
Shows the interior detail of a product.
Orthographic drawing
Shows dimensions and proportions in 2D views (elevation, end elevation, plan).
Exploded view
Shows how components fit together.
Working drawing
Gives the manufacturer all views, dimensions and technical details needed.
Advantage of manual sketching
Quick and free early on; no training or investment.
Advantage of CAD graphics
Easy to edit; many drawings from one model; links to CAM.

Quick check

1. Which graphic best shows the inside of a product?

  1. Exploded view
  2. Sectional view
  3. One-point perspective
  4. Mood board
Show answer
B: Sectional view. A section cuts through to reveal interior detail.

2. Which graphic communicates dimensions most clearly?

  1. Rendered illustration
  2. Orthographic drawing
  3. Mood board
  4. Freehand thumbnail
Show answer
B: Orthographic drawing. Orthographic views are designed to be dimensioned.

3. Why would a designer show a client a rendered 3D illustration rather than an orthographic drawing?

  1. It includes all dimensions
  2. Non-experts find it easier to understand
  3. It is quicker than sketching
  4. It is needed for CNC
Show answer
B: Non-experts find it easier to understand. Realistic 3D views are easier for non-specialists to read.

4. Which is an advantage of CAD over manual drawing?

  1. No training needed
  2. Changes can be made without redrawing
  3. Cheaper equipment
  4. Better at very rough early ideas
Show answer
B: Changes can be made without redrawing. Editing a model updates every view.

Exam-style questions

1. Describe two benefits of CAD drawings compared with manual drawings. (2 marks)

Show marking guidance
  • Easier and quicker to edit if the client wants changes (1)
  • More realistic, so easier for non-experts to understand (1)
  • Can be sent electronically/linked to CAM (1)

2. Explain why different graphic techniques are used at different stages of the design process. (4 marks)

Show marking guidance
  • Early: quick freehand sketches to generate/explore many ideas freely (1)
  • Development: 3D/ sectional / exploded views to resolve form, interior details and assembly (1)
  • Refinement/planning: orthographic, dimensioned drawings to communicate manufacturing details (1)
  • Final: renders/CAD to present to client in a realistic way (1)
