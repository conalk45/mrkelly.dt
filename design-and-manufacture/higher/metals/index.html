Ferrous and non-ferrous

  • Ferrous: contain iron, attracted to magnets (except stainless steel), rust unless protected.
  • Non-ferrous: no iron, not magnetic, form a dense oxide layer (tarnish) that slows further corrosion.
  • Alloy: a mixture of a metal with other elements to improve properties (brass = copper + zinc; stainless steel = steel + chromium).

What catches people out

  • Stated properties without saying why they suit the product, or repeated the same property. Property + why it matters for that part, six different points. Higher 2022, 2023, 2025

Quick check

1. Which metal is ferrous?

  1. Aluminium
  2. Brass
  3. Mild steel
  4. Copper
Show answer
C: Mild steel. Steel is mostly iron.

2. Brass is an alloy of copper and…

  1. Tin
  2. Zinc
  3. Iron
  4. Aluminium
Show answer
B: Zinc. Copper + zinc.

3. Which metal suits kitchen cutlery?

  1. Mild steel
  2. Cast iron
  3. Stainless steel
  4. Copper
Show answer
C: Stainless steel. Corrosion resistant and hygienic.

4. Why is aluminium used for drinks cans?

  1. It is magnetic
  2. Lightweight and corrosion resistant
  3. It is brittle
  4. It is cheapest metal
Show answer
B: Lightweight and corrosion resistant. Light and does not rust.

5. Cast iron is unsuitable for a product that must bend because it is…

  1. Ductile
  2. Brittle
  3. Malleable
  4. Lightweight
Show answer
B: Brittle. Brittle materials snap rather than bend.

6. Which metal is used to coat steel food cans?

  1. Tin
  2. Zinc
  3. Copper
  4. Brass
Show answer
A: Tin. Tin plating is non-toxic and resists corrosion.

Exam-style questions

1. The frame of an outdoor bench is stainless steel. Explain why this is suitable. (2 marks)

Show marking guidance
  • Corrosion resistant so it will not rust outdoors (1)
  • Strong/tough so it supports the users' weight (1)
  • Attractive finish needing little maintenance (1)

2. Explain why the materials chosen for these products are suitable. (Six valid points.) (6 marks)

Show marking guidance
  • Explain, don't just state: property + why it matters for that part
  • e.g. ABS footrest is durable so won't crack under weight (1); chemical resistant so easily cleaned (1)
  • e.g. Plastic-coated steel frame: good strength-to-weight (1); coating prevents corrosion (1)
  • No marks for repeating the same property
