Branches of engineering

EngineerWorks on
CivilRoads, bridges, dams, tunnels, railways, buildings' foundations: large infrastructure
StructuralThe strength and stability of structures: calculating forces, choosing materials and sections
MechanicalMachines and moving parts: engines, drive systems, gears, pneumatics, vehicles
ElectricalGeneration and distribution of power: grids, motors, wiring, charging infrastructure
ElectronicCircuits, sensors, microcontrollers and control systems
ChemicalProcesses that change materials: fuels, plastics, batteries, water treatment
EnvironmentalReducing impact on the environment: surveying wildlife, pollution, waste, renewable energy, planning regulations

Don't mix up electrical (power) and electronic (control circuits): the 2025 N5 report flagged this.

Describing an engineer's activity

Questions ask for an activity an engineer would carry out in a project. Give an action plus the specific aspect: "calculate the forces on the mast" (structural), "design the control circuit and select the sensors" (electronic), "test the drive system" (mechanical), "survey wildlife at the slipway site" (environmental). "Design the controller" alone is too vague.

Big projects need an integrated approach: engineers from different branches work together, each bringing specialist knowledge.

The professional engineer in a project HIGHER

  • Engineers design, implement, test and control complex systems and manage budgets and timescales.
  • Communication and teamwork: sharing designs and calculations, reporting to clients, coordinating with other disciplines.
  • Skills and specialist knowledge: e.g. a structural engineer uses knowledge of forces, materials and factors of safety; an environmental engineer understands impact assessment and regulations. The 2025 Higher report noted candidates struggled to name these specific skills.

What catches people out

  • Couldn't describe the skills of structural or environmental engineers. Structural: analysing loads and designing frames and foundations. Environmental: assessing and reducing the impact on air, water and land. H 2025
