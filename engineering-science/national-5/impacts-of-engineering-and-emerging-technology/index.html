Impacts of engineering & emerging technology

5 cards · 4 quiz · 0 exam

Engineering Science · National 5 · mrkellydt.co.uk

Social, economic and environmental impacts

  • Social (people's lives): health, safety, convenience, jobs and skills, noise, community. E.g. an e-bike scheme encourages exercise and makes travel easier.
  • Economic (money): costs to build, run and maintain; jobs created or lost; cheaper running costs. E.g. an air fryer uses less energy so is cheaper to run.
  • Environmental: emissions, pollution, habitats, resources, waste, landscape. E.g. a dam produces electricity without CO₂ but floods habitats.

Most questions want a positive or a negative of a stated type: read which. Single words like "renewable" or "pollution" don't score: say what and why.

Tackling climate change

Link the solution to greenhouse gases: "Solar panels generate electricity from the sun instead of burning fossil fuels (cause), so no CO₂ is released (effect)." The 2024 N5 report: many didn't link climate change to greenhouse gases; "pollution" alone wasn't accepted.

Emerging technology

An emerging technology is new and not yet in commercial use in products. Examples credited by SQA include ammonia-fuelled engines and nuclear (diamond) batteries. Established or already-developing technologies such as AI, electric vehicles, self-driving cars and (from 2025) graphene earn at most 1 mark. Name the technology and explain its effect: "A nuclear diamond battery could last thousands of years, so the device would never need recharging." Naming alone scores nothing.

"Explain" answers

A single cause relevant to the context and a related effect is enough. Two causes, or a cause with an unrelated effect, don't earn full marks.

What catches people out

  • Gave emerging technologies that are already on sale. Choose something still in development, e.g. ammonia-fuelled ship engines or nuclear diamond batteries. N5 2024
  • Didn't link limiting climate change to greenhouse gases. Mention reducing CO₂ or other greenhouse gas emissions. N5 2024

Key terms

Social impactAn effect on people's lives: health, safety, convenience, jobs.
Economic impactAn effect on money: costs, savings, jobs, running costs.
Environmental impactAn effect on the environment: emissions, habitats, resources, waste.
Emerging technologyNew and not yet in commercial use, e.g. ammonia-fuelled engines, nuclear diamond batteries.
Climate change answersLink to greenhouse gases such as CO₂, not just "pollution".

Quick check

1. Which would score as an emerging technology?

  1. Electric cars
  2. Ammonia-fuelled engines
  3. Artificial intelligence
  4. Smartphones
Show answer
B: Ammonia-fuelled engines. New and not yet commercial.

2. Which links a wind farm to tackling climate change correctly?

  1. It is renewable, so it will last for ever
  2. It reduces the pollution caused by cars
  3. It generates electricity without releasing CO₂
  4. It is quiet and doesn't spoil the landscape
Show answer
C: It generates electricity without releasing CO₂. Mention greenhouse gases.

3. "Cheaper running costs for the user" is a…

  1. Social impact
  2. Structural impact
  3. Environmental impact
  4. Economic impact
Show answer
D: Economic impact. Money.

4. A full "explain" answer needs…

  1. A cause and a related effect
  2. Two causes of the same problem
  3. A list of facts about the topic
  4. A labelled diagram of the system
Show answer
A: A cause and a related effect. One cause + related effect.
