Drive systems & mechanisms

6 cards · 4 quiz · 0 exam

Engineering Science · National 5 · mrkellydt.co.uk

Types of motion

A mechanism changes an input motion and force into a different output motion and force: it makes a job easier. Learn the four motions and their British Standard symbols.

  • Rotary: turning in a circle (a wheel).
  • Linear: in a straight line (a conveyor belt load).
  • Reciprocating: backwards and forwards in a straight line (a pneumatic piston, a saw).
  • Oscillating: swinging backwards and forwards in an arc (a pendulum, a windscreen wiper).

Gear trains

  • In a simple gear train meshing gears turn in opposite directions.
  • Velocity ratio VR = speed of input ÷ speed of output = teeth on driven gear ÷ teeth on driver gear. A VR of 4:1 means the output turns 4 times slower.
  • Speed method: input speed × input teeth = output speed × output teeth. E.g. 1500 × 52 = output × 12, so output = 6500 revs min⁻¹.
  • An idler gear between driver and driven makes them turn in the same direction. It doesn't change the velocity ratio, whatever its size: changing its number of teeth has no effect on the output speed.
  • A compound gear train has two gears fixed on the same shaft, giving a large speed change in a small space. Total VR = VR1 × VR2.

Write speeds in revs min⁻¹ (not "RPM") and simplify ratios, e.g. 120:820 = 6:41. The 2024 N5 report: candidates using the data booklet speed method scored better on compound trains than those juggling ratios.

Speeds, units and compound trains

  • The input gear is the driver; the output gear is the driven.
  • Rotary speed is measured in revolutions per minute: revs min⁻¹.
  • VR = teeth on driven ÷ teeth on driver = input speed ÷ output speed. The SQA calls it the velocity ratio (or speed ratio). Example: driver 14T, driven 72T: VR = 72 ÷ 14 = 5.1, so a 100 revs min⁻¹ input gives about 19 revs min⁻¹.
  • A small driver with a large driven reduces speed; a large driver with a small driven increases it.
  • An idler makes the driven turn the same way as the driver. Its size has no effect on speed, so idlers are kept small to save space and material.
  • In a compound gear train two gears share one shaft and turn at the same speed. Total VR = VR of pair 1 × VR of pair 2. Example: A 20T drives B 60T; C 10T (on B's shaft) drives D 80T: VR = 3 × 8 = 24, so 100 revs min⁻¹ in gives 4.2 revs min⁻¹ out.
  • Compound trains give a large VR in a compact space, with fewer and smaller gears than a simple train.

Friction

In a gear system the meshing teeth rub, turning kinetic energy into heat and sound. Heat makes parts expand, increasing wear and damage, and the lost energy reduces efficiency. Reduce friction with oil or grease, bearings (which change rubbing into rolling), and regular maintenance to replace worn parts.

Friction is not always a problem: it is useful in brakes, clutches and belt drives, where it lets one surface grip another.

What catches people out

  • Didn't state the effect on output speed when an idler gear's teeth were changed. An idler doesn't change the speed or VR, only the direction. Say so clearly. N5 2024

Key terms

Reciprocating motionBackwards and forwards in a straight line.
Oscillating motionSwinging backwards and forwards in an arc.
Velocity ratioSpeed of input ÷ speed of output = driven teeth ÷ driver teeth.
Gear speed methodInput speed × input teeth = output speed × output teeth.
Idler gearMakes driver and driven turn the same way; doesn't change the VR.

Quick check

1. A pneumatic piston moving in and out shows…

  1. Rotary motion
  2. Oscillating motion
  3. Reciprocating motion
  4. Linear only
Show answer
C: Reciprocating motion. Back and forth in a straight line.

2. A 20-tooth driver turns a 60-tooth gear. The VR is…

  1. 1:3
  2. 3:1
  3. 20:1
  4. 60:1
Show answer
B: 3:1. 60 ÷ 20.

3. Reducing the teeth on an idler gear makes the output…

  1. Faster
  2. Slower
  3. No different
  4. Reverse
Show answer
C: No different. Idlers don't change the VR.

4. A 1500 revs min⁻¹, 52-tooth gear drives a 12-tooth gear. Output speed is about…

  1. 346 revs min⁻¹
  2. 6500 revs min⁻¹
  3. 1500 revs min⁻¹
  4. 780 revs min⁻¹
Show answer
B: 6500 revs min⁻¹. 1500 × 52 ÷ 12.
