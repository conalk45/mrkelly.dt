Making & trades

How to become a joiner / carpenter

Makes and fits doors, windows, stairs, roofs and kitchens on site or in a workshop.

What the job involves

  • Reading drawings and marking out
  • Cutting and machining timber
  • Fitting on site
  • Working to building regulations

Useful school subjects

Practical Woodworking, Design & Manufacture. Higher Maths and English help with most routes.

How to get in

Modern Apprenticeship in Carpentry and Joinery (usually 4 years, paid).

Modern ApprenticeshipCollege HNC/HND

Usually entered through a Modern Apprenticeship or college course: search vacancies on apprenticeships.scot ↗.

Related careers

  • Fabricator / welder
  • CNC machinist / engineering technician
  • Model maker / prop maker
