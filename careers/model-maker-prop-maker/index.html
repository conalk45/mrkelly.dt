How to become a model maker / prop maker

Builds models and props for architects, designers, museums, film, theatre and TV.

What the job involves

  • Interpreting drawings and briefs
  • Laser cutting, 3D printing, carving and casting
  • Painting and finishing
  • Working to tight deadlines

Useful school subjects

Design & Manufacture, Practical Woodworking, Creative Thinking. Higher Maths and English help with most routes.

How to get in

HND 3D Design: Model Making (City of Glasgow College), or a design degree.
