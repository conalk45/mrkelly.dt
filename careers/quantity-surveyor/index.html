Buildings & construction

How to become a quantity surveyor

Manages the costs of building projects, from first estimates to final accounts.

What the job involves

  • Measuring drawings and estimating costs
  • Managing budgets and contracts
  • Visiting sites
  • Negotiating with contractors

Useful school subjects

Graphic Communication, Engineering Science. Higher Maths and English help with most routes.

How to get in

Degree in quantity surveying. Graduate Apprenticeships in construction exist.
