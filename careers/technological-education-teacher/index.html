Teaching

How to become a technological education teacher

Teaches Design & Manufacture, Graphic Communication, Engineering Science and practical subjects in secondary schools.

What the job involves

  • Teaching practical and theory lessons
  • Supporting pupils with folios and projects
  • Running the workshop safely
  • Preparing pupils for SQA / QS exams

Useful school subjects

Design & Manufacture, Graphic Communication, Engineering Science, Practical Woodworking, Practical Metalworking, Creative Thinking. Higher Maths and English help with most routes.

How to get in

BSc Technological Education (UHI Perth), Strathclyde's HND-to-degree route, or any relevant degree then a PGDE. Registration with GTC Scotland is required.
