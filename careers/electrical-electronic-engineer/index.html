Engineering

How to become an electrical / electronic engineer

Designs power systems, circuits and control systems, from the national grid to the electronics in phones and cars.

What the job involves

  • Designing and simulating circuits
  • Programming microcontrollers
  • Testing and fault-finding
  • Writing specifications

Useful school subjects

Engineering Science. Higher Maths and English help with most routes.

How to get in

Degree in electrical and electronic engineering, or an apprenticeship and HNC/HND.
