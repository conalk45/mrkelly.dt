Design

  • Product designerDesigns everyday products, from furniture and kitchenware to medical devices, balancing how they look, work…
  • Product design engineerCombines design and engineering: makes sure a product looks good and also works, is strong enough and can be…
  • Graphic designerCommunicates ideas visually: branding, packaging, posters, websites, signage and infographics.
  • UX / UI designerDesigns how apps and websites work and feel, so they are easy and enjoyable to use.
  • Furniture designer-makerDesigns and builds furniture, often as a one-off or small batch for clients.
  • Interior / exhibition designerPlans the inside of spaces, such as shops, museums, offices and homes, including layout, lighting, materials…
  • Jewellery designerDesigns and makes jewellery and small metal objects, by hand or with CAD and 3D printing.

Engineering

  • CAD technician / design engineerTurns engineers' and designers' ideas into accurate 3D models and drawings that can be manufactured or built.
  • Mechanical engineerDesigns and develops machines and moving parts, from engines and wind turbines to medical equipment.
  • Civil engineerPlans and designs roads, bridges, railways, water and flood defences, and manages them being built.
  • Structural engineerMakes sure buildings and bridges stand up safely, calculating the loads and choosing materials and sizes.
  • Electrical / electronic engineerDesigns power systems, circuits and control systems, from the national grid to the electronics in phones and…
  • Robotics / mechatronics engineerBuilds systems that combine mechanics, electronics and software, such as robots, drones and automated…
  • Aerospace engineerDesigns and tests aircraft, satellites and their parts.
  • Renewable energy engineerDesigns, builds and maintains wind, tidal, solar and hydrogen energy systems: a big growth area in Scotland.
  • Manufacturing engineerWorks out the best way to make products in quantity: quickly, accurately and with little waste.
  • Environmental engineerReduces the impact of engineering on air, water and land, including pollution control, recycling and flood…

Buildings & construction

  • ArchitectDesigns buildings and spaces that are beautiful, practical and sustainable, and leads the design team.
  • Architectural technologistThe technical expert on a building design: details how it will be built, chooses materials and makes sure it…
  • Quantity surveyorManages the costs of building projects, from first estimates to final accounts.
  • Construction project managerRuns building projects on site, managing people, safety, time and money.

Making & trades

  • Joiner / carpenterMakes and fits doors, windows, stairs, roofs and kitchens on site or in a workshop.
  • Fabricator / welderCuts, shapes and joins metal to build structures, vehicles, ships and offshore equipment.
  • CNC machinist / engineering technicianSets up and runs CNC lathes and milling machines to make precise parts.
  • Model maker / prop makerBuilds models and props for architects, designers, museums, film, theatre and TV.
