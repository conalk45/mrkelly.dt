How to become a CNC machinist / engineering technician

Sets up and runs CNC lathes and milling machines to make precise parts.

What the job involves

  • Reading engineering drawings
  • Programming and setting CNC machines
  • Measuring parts accurately
  • Maintaining machines

Useful school subjects

Practical Metalworking, Design & Manufacture, Engineering Science. Higher Maths and English help with most routes.

How to get in

Modern Apprenticeship in Engineering (SCQF 6–7).

Modern ApprenticeshipCollege HNC/HND

Usually entered through a Modern Apprenticeship or college course: search vacancies on apprenticeships.scot ↗.
