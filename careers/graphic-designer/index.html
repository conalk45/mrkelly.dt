How to become a graphic designer

Communicates ideas visually: branding, packaging, posters, websites, signage and infographics.

What the job involves

  • Meeting clients and agreeing a brief
  • Creating layouts, logos and typography
  • Using Adobe and other DTP software
  • Preparing files for print and screen

Useful school subjects

Graphic Communication, Creative Thinking. Higher Maths and English help with most routes.

How to get in

Degree or HND in graphic design or communication design. A strong portfolio matters most.
