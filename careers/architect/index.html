Buildings & construction

How to become an architect

Designs buildings and spaces that are beautiful, practical and sustainable, and leads the design team.

What the job involves

  • Sketching and modelling building ideas
  • Producing drawings and 3D visuals
  • Meeting clients and planners
  • Visiting sites during construction

Useful school subjects

Graphic Communication, Design & Manufacture, Creative Thinking. Higher Maths and English help with most routes.

How to get in

A long route: usually a 4-year degree, a year in practice, then 2 more years (or a 5–6 year integrated MArch), plus a professional exam.
