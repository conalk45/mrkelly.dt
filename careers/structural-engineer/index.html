Engineering

How to become a structural engineer

Makes sure buildings and bridges stand up safely, calculating the loads and choosing materials and sizes.

What the job involves

  • Analysing loads and forces
  • Choosing beams, columns and foundations
  • Working with architects
  • Checking work on site

Useful school subjects

Engineering Science. Higher Maths and English help with most routes.

How to get in

Degree in civil or structural engineering.
