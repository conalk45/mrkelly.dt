Engineering

How to become a robotics / mechatronics engineer

Builds systems that combine mechanics, electronics and software, such as robots, drones and automated factories.

What the job involves

  • Designing mechanisms and sensors
  • Programming control systems
  • Building and testing prototypes
  • Improving automated production lines

Useful school subjects

Engineering Science, Design & Manufacture. Higher Maths and English help with most routes.

How to get in

Degree in robotics, mechatronics or electrical and mechanical engineering.
