Engineering

How to become a renewable energy engineer

Designs, builds and maintains wind, tidal, solar and hydrogen energy systems: a big growth area in Scotland.

What the job involves

  • Assessing sites for wind or tidal power
  • Designing energy systems
  • Monitoring performance
  • Working offshore or on site

Useful school subjects

Engineering Science, Design & Manufacture. Higher Maths and English help with most routes.

How to get in

Engineering degree (mechanical, electrical or energy), or a technician apprenticeship.
